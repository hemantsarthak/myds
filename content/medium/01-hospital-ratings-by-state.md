# Which US states have the best-rated hospitals? (CMS data, 2026)

*Draft for Medium. Paste into the editor; links are to the live analyses.*

---

Medicare publishes a star rating for almost every hospital in the United States: one to five stars, built from five groups of measures — mortality, safety of care, readmission, patient experience and timely care. Each measure is compared with what CMS expects for hospitals treating similar patients, then combined into the overall rating.

I aggregated those ratings by state, from the public CMS Provider Data Catalog. The differences are much larger than the national average suggests.

## The top of the table

**Utah** has the highest share of four- and five-star hospitals: about **79%** of its rated hospitals. Colorado follows at 73%, Wisconsin at 64%, and several New England and Mountain West states sit in the high 50s.

At the other end, **Mississippi** has about **13%** of rated hospitals at four stars or better. Several southern and rural states sit between 15% and 30%.

The full table — 42 states with at least 20 rated hospitals — is here: [hospital star ratings by state](https://tekify.xyz/insights/hospital-ratings-by-state/).

## What the numbers actually mean

A few cautions matter more than the ranking itself.

**Unrated is not bad.** Small, rural and specialty hospitals often report too few measures to be rated. Excluding them from the averages is correct statistically, but it also means the states with the most unrated hospitals are the states where the rated set is least representative.

**The share of top-rated hospitals and the average rating are different measures.** A state where most hospitals are unrated can post a flattering average from a small rated set. That is why the table shows both the rated count and the percentage at four stars or better.

**Ownership matters, but not the way people assume.** Grouping by CMS ownership type, non-profit hospitals average slightly higher ratings than for-profit and government hospitals — but the overlap between the groups is large. You can compare the groups here: [hospital ratings by ownership](https://tekify.xyz/insights/hospitals-by-ownership/).

## How to use this

If you are choosing a hospital, the state average is not the useful number. Open the hospital's own page — its measure groups, its emergency-services flag, its ownership — and read the groups separately. A hospital can be strong on survival and weak on readmission.

Every hospital in the country, with its rating, measure groups and contact details, is in a free directory built from the same CMS data: [US Hospitals](https://hospitals.tekify.xyz).
