# HIGH_CPC.md — high-commercial-value content plan (added 2026-10-24)

Human directive (2026-10-24): *"create content on high ppc stuff … don't idle, target is $1k USD in
the first 30 days ASAP and mostly almost fully managed by you, similar to tekify.xyz."*

## Honest assessment first

$1,000 in 30 days (by ~2026-11-21) is **not reachable by SEO/content alone** from where we are:
the domain is 3 days old, has **zero human traffic**, only just started being indexed, and ranks
for nothing yet. Content creates no revenue until it (a) ranks, (b) is seen by humans, and (c) is
monetized at a decent RPM. (a)–(b) take weeks for a new domain no matter what we publish. High-CPC
keywords make this **harder**, not easier — the CPC is high *because* established sites compete.

Ad revenue math: at a good high-CPC RPM of $10/1,000 pageviews, $1,000/mo needs 100,000 human
pageviews/mo (≈3,300/day). At Hilltop's low RPM ($1–3) it needs 330k–1M/mo. We have ~0.

## What actually determines the 30-day outcome

| Lever | Agent can do? | Human needed | Time to money |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEO content (any CPC) | yes, fully | no | rankings 2–6 weeks, revenue after |
| **AdSense** (the only high-CPC ad network that accepts our scale) | prepares site, adds code | **10 min: create AdSense, add site** | needs traffic first anyway |
| Marketplace/service income (Fiverr/Upwork etc.) | **does the work/delivery** | **15 min: create account + KYC/payout** | **days — only credible 30-day path** |
| Paid-ads arbitrage | can run | needs budget + card | days, but high risk of loss |
| Affiliate (high-ticket, e.g. senior-care/insurance leads) | applies, builds pages | program approval may need traffic | weeks |

**Decision taken:** keep building the SEO/content machine (long-term asset, fully autonomous) and
stop pretending it can hit $1k by Nov 21. If the human wants a real 30-day shot, the marketplace
path is the only one — the agent can do 100% of the work; the human only creates the account and
pays out.

## High-CPC content build (agent-managed, starts now)

Target clusters where we already own data/IP and where CPC is high:

1. **Elder-care / Medicare cluster** (fastest — reuses CMS data already in `sites/`):
   - nursinghomes / homehealth / hospitals / dialysis sites → add "cost of care", "Medicare
     coverage", "how to choose", "state-by-state comparison" guides and calculators.
   - New pages: "Medicare-certified home health agencies in <city>", "nursing homes in <county>
     with ratings", "dialysis centers near <city>".
   - Monetization: AdSense (high RPM in senior care), senior-care lead affiliates later.
2. **Health-insurance marketplace cluster** (new subdomain, QHP county data): plans/premiums by
   county — very high CPC; data acquisition needs work (data.healthcare.gov JS portal).
3. **Doctors/dentists directory** (NPPES API is keyless) — huge long-tail, high CPC; heavy.
4. **Finance/insurance calculators** on calculators.tekify.xyz (mortgage, refinance, life
   insurance, debt) with affiliate slots ready for when a finance network approves us.

Rule: no thin autogenerated pages without a genuine data payload + hand-written explainer on top;
AdSense rejects "auto-generated content" sites, and Google's helpful-content system suppresses them.
