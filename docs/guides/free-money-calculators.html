The money calculators worth bookmarking

Loan payments, mortgage costs, retirement withdrawal, debt payoff, take-home pay and compound growth — each with the formula shown, so you can check the answer yourself.

Most financial questions reduce to a handful of formulas. These calculators show the arithmetic on the page — the formula, a worked example and the assumptions — so you can verify the result instead of trusting a black box.

Borrowing

  • Loan and mortgage payments — monthly payment, total interest and an amortisation schedule.
  • Refinance break-even — how many months before the lower rate pays off the closing costs.
  • Debt payoff — how long a balance takes to clear and what extra payments save.

Earning and saving

  • Take-home pay — federal brackets, FICA and the standard deduction.
  • Self-employment tax — the 15.3% both-halves calculation for 1099 income.
  • Compound interest and savings goals — what a monthly contribution becomes.

Retirement

Retirement withdrawal (the 4% rule), 401(k) growth, dividend income and the nest egg needed for a target income.
